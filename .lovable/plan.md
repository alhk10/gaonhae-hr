# Printable forms: Perth Seminars & Poomsae Championship 2026

Two print-ready A4 PDFs saved in Files (no app changes). Gaonhae logo, all details taken from the existing /perthcomp rules.

## 1. Individual Registration Form (1–2 pages)
- Event header: Scarborough Community Centre 2 – Function Room; Sat 28 Nov seminars, Sun 29 Nov Poomsae competition. Open to all clubs.
- Participant details: full name, certificate name, date of birth, gender, club/school, current belt/grade (geup or Poom/Dan), parent/guardian name, phone, email.
- Seminars (tick boxes): PS1, KS1, PS2, KS2 with times and who can join.
- Competition: up to 2 events, age division by birth year (5–6, 7–8, 9–11, 12–14, 15–17, 18+), compete at own grade or higher, Poom/Dan only in Poom/Dan events.
- Fees box: $45 per seminar/event, Gaonhae students $40 (GST incl.), no spectator fee; total due line.
- Payment details and proof-of-payment note; deadline 6 Nov 2026 23:59 (Perth).
- Waiver/consent with signature and date lines (parent signature for under 18).
- Office-use box: received date, paid/verified, entered by.
- Enquiries: perth.gaonhaetaekwondo@gmail.com.

## 2. Club / School Collation Form (landscape)
- Page 1: club/school name, head instructor, contact phone/email, address, number of participants, total fee, payment reference, instructor declaration and signature.
- Pages 2–3: roster table, ~15 rows per page, numbered: Name, DOB, Gender, Belt/Grade, Division, PS1, KS1, PS2, KS2, Event 1, Event 2, Fee.
- Per-page subtotal and grand total lines; quick-reference key of seminar codes, divisions and rules at the bottom.

## Technical
- Generated with Python reportlab into `/mnt/documents/perth-comp-2026-individual-form.pdf` and `perth-comp-2026-club-collation-form.pdf`.
- Each page rendered to image and checked for clipping/alignment before delivery.
- Bank/PayNow details: will use the Australian branch payment details from the saved invoice template if found; otherwise left as a blank line to fill in.
